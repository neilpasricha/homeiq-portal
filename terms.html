What HomeIQ is

HomeIQ is a home-records app: a private place for your household to keep equipment details, documents, maintenance schedules, expenses, and trusted contractors.

Automatic document details

Document organizing can misread or omit information. Proposed model numbers, serial numbers, dates, prices, warranties, and work history are not authoritative until you compare them with the source and choose to add them. You are responsible for reviewing each proposal.

Subscriptions

Your home record is free. HomeIQ Pro adds shared lists, the house map (including its 3D view), and finding home details in your documents with AI. One subscription covers everyone in a home while the subscriber is a member of it. Pro is billed monthly or yearly, as you choose, through the App Store or Google Play at the price shown before you confirm. You must be 18, or have a parent’s permission, to subscribe. New subscribers may get the first month free when the store offers it: when the free month ends, the plan you chose starts and is charged automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the free month ends. The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends, in your Apple ID or Google Play subscription settings; cancelling stops the next renewal, and you keep access until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies. If we change the price, the store tells you before it applies to you. If Pro ends, shared lists and your map’s floors and pins stay readable, and records added from your documents stay yours, but the 3D view and reading new documents with AI need Pro. Whether or not you subscribe, your records stay readable, and the home summary stays exportable.
