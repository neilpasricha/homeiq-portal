Privacy policy

Last updated September 28, 2026

What HomeIQ stores

HomeIQ keeps the home records you and your household create: homes and their addresses, areas, equipment, documents and photos, maintenance schedules and history, expenses, contractors, shared lists, and your house map (its floors, their drawings, and the spots you pin), along with your account email and display name. When you trace a photo of a floor plan or scan a room with your phone’s camera, the photo or scan is processed on your phone and only the resulting walls are saved; the photo and the scan are not uploaded. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your email address and, if you choose to share it, your name — nothing else from those accounts. If you subscribe to HomeIQ Pro, Apple or Google tells us your subscription’s status and renewal date through our subscription service provider, RevenueCat, which receives the store’s purchase records and a random account number, not your name or email. We never receive your payment details. Records belong to your household and are only visible to people you invite.

Where it lives

Your records are stored with our hosting provider (Supabase) in a private database and private file storage. Subscription purchases are processed by RevenueCat. Files are never publicly accessible; the app retrieves them with short-lived signed links. Our service providers process data only to run HomeIQ and are bound to protect it at least as carefully as we do.

Optional document organizing

When you choose “Find home details,” HomeIQ sends that one document to our AI processing provider, OpenAI, solely to return proposed home records. HomeIQ does not send your account email or profile with the document. HomeIQ asks for your explicit permission before the first document is sent, records that permission, and never sends a document without it; you can withdraw it any time in Settings. Under OpenAI’s API terms, documents are not used to train its models and are kept for up to 30 days to monitor for abuse, then deleted, unless the law requires longer. Nothing the organizer proposes is added until you review and confirm it, and you can keep using documents without this feature.

What we never do

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share your household records with advertisers. We use service providers only to operate features described here. Local provider searches open in the Maps app you pick and send only your service category and coarse locality (city, state, postal code) — never your street address or contact details.

Analytics

Production product analytics are not enabled in this build. HomeIQ is designed to use a small set of anonymous product events (for example “task created”) if analytics are enabled later. Those events may not include names, addresses, document contents, notes, amounts, or household text.

Deleting your data

When you delete a record (an item, task, expense, contractor, area, list, map floor or map pin), it disappears from the app immediately and is permanently erased from our servers within 30 days. You can delete your account any time from Settings; it also asks RevenueCat to delete its record of your purchases, but it doesn’t cancel a subscription, which you cancel in your Apple ID or Google Play settings. Homes only you belong to and their database records are permanently deleted; stored files are committed to a restricted cleanup queue that retries until removal succeeds. In homes you share, records remain with the household but are no longer attributed to you. Account deletion cannot be undone.
