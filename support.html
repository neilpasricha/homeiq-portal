Get help

Email neil@pasricha.ai and a real person answers — usually the same day. Tell me what you were doing and what happened; screenshots help.

Your data

You can export a summary of your home's records from Settings at any time, and delete your account from Settings too. Your account and records are deleted straight away and cannot be recovered; stored files follow through a cleanup queue shortly after. You can also request deletion without the app — see what deletion covers.

HomeIQ Pro

Your home record is free. HomeIQ Pro adds live shared lists, the house map and reading documents with AI, for everyone in your home. It is billed by the App Store (Google Play later), and new subscribers get the first month free. To cancel, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone at least 24 hours before the renewal or the end of the free month. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Deleting your HomeIQ account does not cancel a subscription.
