Delete your HomeIQ account

HomeIQ, by Neil Pasricha.

In the app

Open HomeIQ, go to the Settings tab, and tap Delete account. Type DELETE to confirm. If you sign in with Apple, Apple asks you to confirm once more so HomeIQ can disconnect itself from your Apple ID.

Deleting your account does not cancel a HomeIQ Pro subscription. Cancel it first in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions (or in Google Play).

Without the app

Email neil@pasricha.ai from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my HomeIQ account”. We reply to confirm it is you and delete the account within 7 days of that confirmation.

What is deleted

  • Your account, email address and profile.
  • Every home that only you belong to, with all of its items, tasks, history, expenses, contractors, lists, essentials, documents and house map (floors, drawings, pins). Stored files are removed by a cleanup queue that retries until they are gone.
  • The record of your HomeIQ Pro purchases held by our subscription processor, RevenueCat.
  • Records you deleted earlier are already erased within 30 days of deleting them.

What is kept

In homes you share with other people, the records stay with that household, because they are theirs too, but they are no longer attributed to you. HomeIQ keeps no other copy of your data after deletion.
